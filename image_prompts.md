## Tips for Using These Prompts

1. **For Midjourney:** Add `--ar 3:2` for landscape orientation matching the PDF layout, and `--style raw` for cleaner lines
2. **For DALL-E 3:** These prompts work well as-is
3. **For Stable Diffusion:** Add negative prompt: `color, shading, gradient, filled areas, realistic, photograph, 3D`
4. **Resolution:** Generate at minimum 2480 x 1654 pixels (A4 landscape at 300 DPI) for print quality
5. **Post-processing:** You may want to increase contrast and ensure pure white backgrounds for best coloring book results
