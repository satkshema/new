# Coloring Book Image Prompts: Ornate Anatomy
## "Organs, Cells & Systems" — Decorative Illustration Style

**Theme:** Organs, cells, and systems rendered in ornate, decorative illustration styles (art nouveau lungs, mandala kidneys, botanical-style nervous system). Educational yet beautiful — filling the gap between clinical diagrams and children's formats.

**Style Notes for All Pages:**
- Black line art on white background, suitable for coloring
- Highly detailed with intricate patterns
- Mix of Art Nouveau, mandala, botanical, and decorative illustration styles
- Anatomically educational yet artistically beautiful
- Thick outlines for main structures, fine lines for internal detail
- Leave generous white space within sections for coloring

---

## Production Notes
- **Target size:** 8.5" x 11" (letter) or A4
- **Line weight:** Primary outlines 0.8-1.2pt, secondary detail 0.3-0.5pt
- **Recommended tools:** Fine-tip markers, colored pencils, or gel pens
- **Difficulty level:** Advanced adult coloring — high detail density
- **Educational value:** Each page teaches real anatomy through artistic interpretation
