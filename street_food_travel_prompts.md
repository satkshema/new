## Production Notes for AI Image Generators

### Recommended Settings:
- **Midjourney:** Add `--ar 3:4` (portrait) or `--ar 1:1` (square) for book pages, `--style raw` for cleaner lines
- **DALL-E 3:** Prompts work as-is; emphasize "black ink line drawing, no shading, coloring book style"
- **Stable Diffusion XL:** Use negative prompt: `color, shading, gradient, watercolor, photorealistic, 3D render, blurry, low detail, fills`
- **Resolution:** Generate at minimum 2480×3508 px (A4 at 300 DPI) for print quality

### Visual Consistency Tips:
1. Add a recurring visual element across all pages (e.g., a small bird, the chef's hat, or steam swirls) for series cohesion
2. Use the same line-weight description in every prompt
3. Keep the dish at consistent foreground prominence (~30-40% of frame)
4. Consider adding the dish name in decorative typography as a label/banner

### Optional Page Additions:
- **Recipe sidebar** on facing page (text-only)
- **"Local pronunciation guide"** in decorative ribbon
- **Map dot** showing the country/city on a small world map corner
- **Cultural note** in italic script

### Marketing Angle:
> "Travel the world from your couch — color the dishes, color the streets, taste the journey."

Perfect for: travel enthusiasts, foodies, mindfulness coloring market, gift-givers, culinary students, adult coloring book buyers tired of generic mandalas.
